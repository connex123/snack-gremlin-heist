# Taking the four screenshots (Roblox Studio)

The shot keys only work in Roblox Studio. Each key sets up its scene through the game's real systems
(cash, stall, belt, event, surge, pirates, lock) and then fixes the camera. Nothing needs to be played first.

## Steps

1. Open the place in Roblox Studio: **Snack Gremlin Heist** (place 87862033840794). Maximise the Studio
   window, then open **View > Output** so you can see the log.
2. Press **Play** (F5, the solo Play button, not "Run").
3. Wait until the market has loaded and your character is standing in front of the yellow stall. The
   Output should show `[SnackGremlinHeist] server ready` and
   `[ShotDirector] ready: press 1 cover, 2 buy, 3 threat, 4 progress, 0 back to play`.
   Give it about 5 more seconds.
4. **Click once on the 3D game view**, so the keyboard goes to the game and not to the Explorer or the chat.
5. Press **1**, wait **4 seconds**, and capture the game view → `1-cover.jpg`.
6. Press **2**, wait **4 seconds**, and capture → `2-buy.jpg`.
7. Press **3**, wait **4 seconds**, and capture → `3-threat.jpg`.
8. Press **4**, wait **4 seconds**, and capture → `4-progress.jpg`.
9. Press **0** to get the normal camera back (optional), then Stop.

Each key prints `[ShotDirector] staged <name>` in the Output. If a shot looks off, press its key again
and wait 4 seconds. Shots can be taken in any order.

## What each shot should show

**1 — cover.** A view from above the lane, looking down into your stall. It should show:
- Eight gremlins on white pedestals: five in front, three behind.
- A three-line label above each gremlin: a white name, the rarity in its colour, and a green `+$…/s`.
  No label overlaps another.
- Three mutations that glow: **Gold** Jollof Jetpack (golden outline), **Rainbow** Generator Seraph
  (colour-cycling) and **Neon** Plantain Pharaoh (pink).
- Your character standing at the right-hand edge of the stall.
- Top left, under Roblox's buttons: the cash panel showing about **$48M**, a green `+$…/s` rate and
  `STALL 8/10 · REBIRTH x2.0`.
- Top centre: an orange **JOLLOF RAIN!** banner with a countdown.
- Top right: the game title. Bottom: the five buttons.
- Orange "jollof" rain falling, and market buildings beyond the stall's back wall.

**2 — buy.** Your character on the lane beside the Snack Belt, seen from behind. It should show:
- A **Danfo Dragon (Legendary)** held above your head, with its label.
- Three more gremlins on the belt with price lines (Kilishi Knight; a red Pepper Okra Octopus; a teal
  Lagoon Chin-Chin Cheetah).
- A **CARRYING: RUN HOME!** card under the cash panel. No event banner in this shot.

**3 — threat.** A view from the west end of the lane. It should show:
- A blue **LAGOON SURGE INCOMING!** banner with a countdown.
- On the right, your stall front covered by a glowing blue shutter (`LOCKED …s`), with a **Pocket
  Pirate** in front of it labelled **LOCKED OUT!**.
- Your character next to that pirate, facing it.
- Across the belt, a second pirate (`STEALING!`) holding a gold Ogbono Overlord at the rival stall.
- Far back on the left, the teal and white surge wave rising out of the lagoon.

**4 — progress.** It should show:
- The **UPGRADES** panel on the left (four upgrades with levels and costs, plus the purple Rebirth box).
- The **GREMLIN INDEX** on the right (found and total per rarity, with coloured bars).
- Your stall and character between the two panels.
- A red **PEPPER HOUR!** banner at the top.

## If something goes wrong

- **A key does nothing.** Click the 3D view once more (the chat box or the Explorer may have the keyboard),
  then press the key again.
- **Any red text or a yellow warning in the Output** (lines starting with `[SnackGremlinHeist]` or
  `[ShotDirector]`, or anything red): select everything in the Output window (Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C) and send
  it back, with the screenshot that looked wrong and the key you pressed. Each system starts separately,
  so the rest of the game keeps running after one error and the other shots may still work. Take them anyway.
